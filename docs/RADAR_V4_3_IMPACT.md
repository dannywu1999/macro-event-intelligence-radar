# V4.3 Visual Impact Intelligence MVP

The existing read-only API adds `impact_intelligence`, contract
`IMPACT_HYPOTHESES_V0`, keyed by original article reference. It changes no source
activation, canonical identity, Official Evidence or market acquisition contract.

## Typed, conditional interpretation

`Impact`, `Mechanism`, `MarketExposure` and bilingual `Text` are TypedDict records.
Each source-attributed headline retains source URL, observed/reported clocks and
its exact canonical membership. Four small rules require explicit causal terms:
monetary policy plus identified central bank; trade restrictions/agreements;
physical crude/oil benchmarks; conflict plus supply/transport channel.
Institution or country context alone cannot create a hypothesis.

Each mechanism includes matched terms, explanation, exposed market rationale,
conditions, counter-scenario and next observations. All mechanism/market evidence
is HYPOTHESIS. Direction is null. There are no importance/confidence/probability
scores, prices, returns, stock predictions or recommendations. WTI/Brent or relevant
yield curves are candidate future observations, not acquired market data. Trade
and route-exposed firms stay unidentified; SPY/QQQ/Gold/DXY are not automatically
assigned. Existing curated institution context is reused for jurisdiction only.

## Evidence boundary

Reported headlines are not independently verified. Conservative headline cues
mark analysis/context or an exactly recognized reported rate decision; all other
article types are Unknown. No headline is labeled a verified new breaking event.
Novelty remains NOT_ESTABLISHED. Commentary or operational guidelines are not
rate decisions. Only exact event-linked OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED nonempty propositions
are shown as official facts; they never confirm the mechanism or market response.
Reaction status stays NOT_MEASURED: no event-attributed empirical comparison is
performed, even if a source headline mentions historical prices. Missing source
publication dates remain Unknown; date proxies retain their explicit label.
Bad provenance, ambiguous event membership or insufficient causal terms yields
IMPACT_NOT_ESTABLISHED with no exposure labels.

## UI and deployment

Recent eligible stories and Explore Impact come before the world map. Source
status is compact; the full source grid is inside a collapsed Data Source Status
section. Map stays before Watch. Full chronological News and existing Event Detail
remain available. News/event Explore Impact opens the exact article's expandable
impact panel inside its existing Event Detail. Diagram and prose read the same
mechanism/exposure objects. Clicking either node reveals rationale, assumptions
and evidence status; no invented chart is drawn. English and Traditional Chinese
use original headlines and presentation-only topic hints.

PREVIEW/FROZEN, replay knowledge time/unavailability, proposition authority,
context versus event geography and NO_MATCH expectation links remain unchanged.
No private history/data, persistence, new provider, framework or trading API is
introduced. Public packaging uses explicit allowlists and reviewed hashes.
